# About this repository

**Text Harvester** extracts handwritten inscriptions from memorial record
sheets, burial registers and grave record cards using vision AI models,
producing structured data for heritage and genealogy archives.

What you are looking at is a **generated snapshot** of that codebase, published
for transparency and reuse. It is produced by an export step from a private
development repository.

## What that means

- **There is no history.** Every publish replaces the tree with a single commit.
  `git log`, `git blame` and `git bisect` will not show development history,
  and that is deliberate: the development repository holds client records and
  internal notes that must not be published.
- **Do not open a pull request here.** It cannot be merged — the next publish
  replaces the whole tree. Please open an issue instead, and the change can be
  applied upstream.
- **Included:** the application, its tests, the evaluation framework with its
  synthetic fixtures, and the user-facing documentation.
- **Not included:** internal operational and incident documentation, and any
  client data. Real ground truth and source images are excluded by design.
